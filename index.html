About Us

Built to open African commodities to the world

Stratum Commodities Ltd is a UK-registered commodity trading company established to give extraordinary producers in underserved regions a direct path to European buyers. For too long, families and operators mining, processing, and trading world-class commodities across the Horn of Africa have been held back by brokers, middlemen, and infrastructure gaps that swallow margin and drown quality in noise.

Unlike traditional brokers, we work directly with family-owned production facilities and trusted partners across East Africa. These are relationships built on decades of trust, shared heritage, and mutual accountability, not procurement spreadsheets.

Because we control the supply chain from source to delivery, we eliminate the markups and inefficiencies that come with intermediaries. The result is genuinely competitive pricing on independently verified, high-quality products, delivered to European and global markets with their integrity intact.

We are the commercial and operational arm for a network of family businesses and close associates, each specialising in a specific commodity. That gives us deep expertise, reliable supply, and a single point of accountability across multiple product lines.
